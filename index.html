        How it works

        Same week, every week

        Every week has the same shape. Only the base rate changes, once per level, so the first months feel easy and the habit carries the harder ones.

        The weekly rhythm

          Two climbers, one rope

          Mon–Sat days alternate between you, and the order flips each week so Payday Fridays land evenly. Sundays and Base Camps are shared. Over the year the plan splits almost exactly 50/50, with about a fifth of the total saved together.

          Swap days whenever life needs it. The mountain only counts dollars.

          Why it escalates

          The base rate climbs $46 → $68 a day. Starting light builds the streak while the account is new; by the time the numbers grow, the transfer is a reflex and the balance is motivating on its own.

          In a high-yield account at 4.0% APY the growing balance also earns about $470 by Summit Day.

          Guide notes

          Seven rules from the people who make it to the top.

          1. Automate the floor.

            Open a separate high-yield savings account, name it after the goal, and set one recurring transfer every Friday for the week's total ($368 this week). The taps are the ritual. The transfer is the engine.

          2. Pay the mountain first.

            Move the money the day pay lands, before anything else gets spent. If the week's number isn't there, the budget is the thing to fix, not the challenge.

          3. Let interest climb with you.

            At 4.0% APY the plan's balance earns about $470 by Summit Day, roughly a week of climbing you don't have to do.

          4. Missed a day? Tap it late. Never double the next one.

            Two rules keep the streak honest: never skip two in a row, and Sundays are for catching up.

          5. Windfalls go on the mountain.

            Refunds, bonuses, side income, sold stuff: log each one as a boost the day it arrives, before it finds another job.

          6. Celebrate every camp, cheaply.

            Each altimeter milestone has a ritual under $25. Hitting a milestone and then raiding the fund is how climbs end early.

          7. Keep the summit in view.

            Decide now where the money goes the week you reach it: brokerage, down payment, index fund. Money with a destination is much harder to spend.
